Tagline
"Connecting people with life-saving medicines and healthcare, powered by Artificial Intelligence."
Mission
Build an AI-powered healthcare cross-platform app that enables users to instantly locate medicines, compare prices, check pharmacy availability in real time, receive trusted medication information, and access nearby healthcare services—all through a simple, multilingual interface designed for Africa.
Problem Statement
Access to essential medicines remains a major challenge in many developing countries.
Patients often:
•	Travel long distances only to discover a medicine is out of stock. 
•	Spend hours searching multiple pharmacies. 
•	Receive incorrect information from social media. 
•	Struggle to understand prescriptions. 
•	Cannot identify trusted healthcare facilities. 
•	Delay treatment because they don't know where to go. 
Pharmacies also struggle with:
•	Limited digital visibility. 
•	Manual inventory management. 
•	Inefficient communication with customers. 
The result is wasted time, increased healthcare costs, delayed treatment, and avoidable health risks.
Proposed Solution
Develop an intelligent healthcare ecosystem where citizens can:
•	Search medicines instantly. 
•	View pharmacies with available stock. 
•	Compare medicine prices. 
•	Reserve medicines. 
•	Navigate to pharmacies using maps. 
•	Chat with an AI healthcare assistant. 
•	Understand prescriptions. 
•	Receive medication reminders. 
•	Find hospitals and clinics nearby. 
•	Report medicine shortages. 
•	Access emergency healthcare information. 
Core AI Features
AI Healthcare Assistant
Users can ask questions naturally:
"I have had a fever for three days."
AI should:
•	Explain possible common causes. 
•	Recommend whether symptoms appear mild, urgent, or require emergency care. 
•	Suggest visiting a healthcare provider when appropriate. 
•	Never provide definitive medical diagnoses. 
•	Encourage professional medical consultation for serious symptoms. 

Medicine Information AI
Users ask:
"How should I take Amoxicillin?"
AI explains:
•	Purpose 
•	Usage instructions 
•	Common side effects 
•	Storage recommendations 
•	Important precautions 
•	When to seek medical advice 
Prescription Scanner
Users upload a prescription.
AI:
•	Extracts medicine names using OCR. 
•	Recognizes dosages. 
•	Explains each medicine. 
•	Searches nearby pharmacies with available stock. 
Image Recognition
Users photograph:
•	Medicine packaging 
•	Prescription 
•	Tablet strip 
AI identifies:
•	Medicine name 
•	Manufacturer 
•	Usage 
•	Safety information 
•	Possible counterfeit warning indicators (with appropriate disclaimers)
